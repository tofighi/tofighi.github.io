Optional: PERT three-point estimator for schedule and cost

Enter optimistic (O), most likely (M) and pessimistic (P) estimates. The PERT-weighted expected estimate is (O + 4M + P) / 6. The standard deviation is (P − O) / 6. Schedule and cost use the same formula but different units. These are planning estimates, not current EVM performance indicators.

EVM formulas & terminology

All measures are calculated as of the selected status date using the approved budget and reported progress of each work package.

PV / BCWS — Planned ValueΣ (BAC × planned %)
